HER RETURN · A 45-DAY BIBLE-STUDY JOURNAL

Come back
without pretending.

This is not a collection of quick quotes. Her Return is a serious, grace-filled pathway for the woman who feels distant, distracted, disappointed, numb, ashamed, or simply hungry for God again. Move through Scripture slowly. Study the context. Tell the truth. Practice what you read. Let consistency grow from communion—not performance.

✝

Grace meets honesty.

Five movements guide the journey: Return, Rooted, Renewed, Wise Love, and Sent. Every day includes biblical context, a substantial lesson, three truths, four reflection questions, a practice, and prayer.
