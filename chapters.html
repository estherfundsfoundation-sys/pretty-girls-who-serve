BEFORE YOU APPLY

Know the pathway.

Submitting an application does not authorize anyone to use the PGWS name, logo, social media identity, collect dues, recruit publicly, or represent a chapter as approved.

1

Discern the mission

PGWS is ministry, leadership, sisterhood, and service—not simply a social club.

2

Choose the right model

Campus chapters follow their institution’s recognition process. Community chapters follow national requirements.

3

Interview with Nationals

We learn who you are, how you lead, and whether service with Christ at the core fits your vision.

4

Wait for written approval

Only a written PGWS Nationals acceptance permits you to begin the official launch pathway.

5

Build and train your board

Approved founders recruit officers, complete training, and prepare governance documents.

6

Launch in good standing

After institutional approval, Nationals provides chapter branding, email, social account support, and recognition.

WHAT NATIONALS PROVIDES

Your chapter will not build alone.

  • Official chapter recognition after approval
  • Founder recognition and launch support
  • Chapter email and approved social-media setup
  • Official logo and brand controls
  • Executive-board training and onboarding
  • Programming, service, meeting, and reporting tools
  • Chapter-status tracking and national support
  • Collaboration on polos, shirts, and approved chapter items
PGWS is an LLC.

PGWS chapters operate within the PGWS structure and support the connected mission of Esther Funds Foundation. Chapters must never present PGWS as a nonprofit or imply that PGWS funds belong to EFF unless an authorized transfer is made and recorded.

SEAMLESS FOUNDER ONBOARDING

One pathway. No guessing.

Applicants move through a clear national pipeline: confirmation, screening, group interview, written decision, founder onboarding, executive-board recruitment, training, university recognition, and official launch.

1

Apply once

Receive a reference number and confirmation email.

2

National screening

Nationals reviews mission, leadership, ministry, and community fit.

3

Group interview

Qualified founders receive the official call invitation and preparation guide.

4

Written decision

Accepted, additional interview, or declined. No one begins without written approval.

5

Founder workspace

Approved founders unlock the checklist, files, training, and chapter tracker in P31.

6

Official launch

After required approvals, Nationals issues the chapter logo, email, and launch support.
